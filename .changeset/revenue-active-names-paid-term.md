Name Growth & Churn counts a .eth name while it is inside a term it paid for. The 62 days ENSv2 added to every v1 name for free are no longer counted, so October and November 2026 show their real net loss instead of a net gain. The count of names still registered on chain is shown next to the headline, and the API returns it as `cumulativeOnchain`.

ENS revenue has an ENSv2 section: revenue since the ENSv2 launch, the share of v1 names migrated, new ENSv2 registrations and renewals, with daily charts of revenue and migration. The API serves the series at `/revenue/ensv2-daily` (set `REVENUE_DUNE_ENSV2_DAILY_QUERY_ID`).
